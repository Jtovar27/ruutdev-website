When you contact us or request a demo through our website, you are providing information directly to RuutDev. We take your privacy seriously.

2 Data We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us through our contact form or direct communication. This may include:

  • Full name — to address you properly
  • Business name — to understand your project context
  • Email address — to respond to your inquiry
  • Business type — to tailor our proposal to your industry
  • Project description — details you share about your needs

We do not collect payment information directly. Any payment processing is handled through third-party services (Zelle, PayPal) with their own privacy policies.

We do not use tracking scripts, analytics platforms, or advertising cookies on this website.

3 How We Use Your Data

The information you provide is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or demo request
  • Prepare a personalized proposal for your business
  • Communicate updates about your project during development
  • Send project files, invoices, or deliverables

We will never use your data for unsolicited marketing, sell it to third parties, or use it for any purpose beyond the service you requested.

4 Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except in the following limited cases:

  • Web3Forms — our contact form processor. They transmit your form submission to our email. See their privacy policy at web3forms.com
  • Legal requirement — if required by law or valid legal process

No other third parties have access to your data.

5 Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to complete the service you requested and for a reasonable period afterward for support purposes — typically no more than 2 years from our last interaction.

If you would like your data deleted sooner, you may request this at any time by contacting us at Tovar_1803@hotmail.com.

6 Your Rights

Regardless of your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to deletion — request that we delete your data
  • Right to object — object to how we use your data
  • Right to portability — receive your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at Tovar_1803@hotmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7 Cookies

Our website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes. The only technical cookies that may be set are strictly necessary session cookies used by our hosting provider (Vercel) to serve the website correctly.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to use our site.

8 Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all data in transit. Form submissions are processed via encrypted channels through Web3Forms.

However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We recommend not including sensitive financial or personal information in contact form messages.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify existing clients by email.
