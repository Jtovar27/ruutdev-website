Payment Structure

How project payments work

We split project payments into milestones so you only pay as work is delivered and approved.

Plan Project Size Payment Schedule Notes
Plan A Under $800 50% deposit 50% on launch Deposit due to start work. Balance due before final delivery.
Plan B $800 – $2,000 40% deposit 30% at delivery 30% on launch Deposit to start. Second payment when draft is delivered. Final on approval and launch.
Plan C Over $2,000 33% deposit 33% at delivery 34% after 30 days Same as Plan B, with final payment 30 days after launch once everything is confirmed working.

Accepted payments: Zelle, PayPal, and bank transfer (USD only). All payments must be in US dollars. Late payments incur a 10% fee after a 14-day grace period. Deposits are non-refundable after work begins.

FAQ

Pricing questions answered

Is maintenance required?

No. Maintenance is completely optional. You can take full ownership of your project after payment is complete and manage it yourself, or opt into a plan for ongoing support.

Can I cancel maintenance anytime?

Yes. You can cancel with 30 days' written notice. The plan will not renew after the current period ends. No refunds are issued for partial months.

Do monthly payments count toward buying my project?

No. Monthly maintenance fees cover ongoing service (hosting, support, updates) — they are not credited toward project ownership. If you wish to buy out a project, that is handled separately in your contract.

What happens if I stop paying maintenance?

After a 14-day grace period, services may be suspended. We'll notify you before any action is taken and work with you to find a resolution. See our Terms & Conditions for full details.

How is the final project price determined?

After our initial conversation, we send you a written proposal with the exact price, timeline, and scope. There are no surprises — you approve everything before work begins.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Zelle, PayPal, and bank transfer in USD. For recurring maintenance, we're also integrating Stripe for automatic billing — available soon.
