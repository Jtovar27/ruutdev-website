FAQ

Questions answered

What qualifies for the $45/month plan?

The $45/mo plan is designed for simple informational websites — up to 5 pages, no complex backend, no e-commerce, no user login. Think: a local restaurant, a freelancer portfolio, a small service business. We confirm eligibility on our first free call. If your project needs more, we'll quote the Standard plan instead.

With the monthly plan, do I own my website?

While you're on the monthly plan, RuutDev retains ownership of the code and IP — we're essentially licensing your site to you while we maintain it. At any time you can exercise the buyout option (price set in your contract) and receive full ownership. After the buyout, the site is 100% yours.

What happens if I cancel the monthly plan without buying out?

With 30 days' written notice, the plan cancels at the end of the billing period. The site goes offline since hosting is part of the plan. You can reactivate, buy out, or transition to a new provider — we'll work with you to make the offboarding smooth.

Which model is better for me — monthly or one-time?

Monthly is great if you want to get started with low upfront cost and have someone handle all the technical side for you. One-time is better if you want to own your project outright and have the budget to pay in milestones. Both models produce the same quality work — the difference is just in ownership and payment structure.

How is the one-time price determined?

After our initial conversation, we send you a written proposal with the exact price, timeline, and scope. There are no surprises — you approve everything before work begins. The prices shown ($400+, $1,200+, $2,500+) are minimums; the exact quote depends on your specific requirements.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Zelle, PayPal, bank transfer (USD), and credit/debit card via Stripe. All payments are in US dollars.
