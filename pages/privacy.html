Section 1

Who We Are

RuutDev is operated by Johan Tovar, a web development and software services business based in Florida, USA, operating at ruutdev.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you interact with our website or engage our services.

Section 2

Data We Collect

We only collect information you provide voluntarily. We do not use tracking pixels, behavioral analytics, or data brokers.

  • Contact form submissions: Name, business name, email, business type, and project description.
  • Payment information: Processed directly by Stripe or other providers. We do not store card numbers.
  • Communications: Emails, WhatsApp messages, and other correspondence you initiate.
  • Project files: Content, images, and brand assets you provide for your project.
Section 3

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to: respond to inquiries; prepare proposals and invoices; deliver and manage your project; process payments; provide maintenance and support; and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, remarketing, or sale to third parties.

Section 4

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Web3Forms: Processes contact form submissions and forwards them to us.
  • Stripe: Handles payment processing under PCI-DSS compliance.
  • Vercel: Hosts our website and processes standard web traffic.
  • Legal authorities: Only when required by law or valid legal process.
Section 5

Data Retention

Contact form data is retained for up to 2 years. Project files and communications are kept for the duration of the project and up to 2 years afterward. Payment records are kept as required by applicable tax law. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 6).

Section 6

Your Rights

You have the right to: access the data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request deletion ("right to be forgotten"); receive your data in a portable format; and object to our processing in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@ruutdev.com. We respond within 30 days.

Section 7

Cookies

We use only what is needed to operate the site, forms, payments, and measurement for our own marketing. Google Tag Manager may load measurement tags for page and conversion events. Third-party services embedded on our site (form processors, payment providers, analytics or ad platforms) may set their own cookies under their respective policies.

We do not sell personal information or use data brokers. If our measurement setup changes materially, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Section 8

Security

All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Form submissions use secure encrypted channels. Payment data is handled by PCI-DSS compliant processors and never stored on our servers.

No electronic transmission or storage method is 100% secure. We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Section 9

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18. If you believe we have done so inadvertently, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. For significant changes, active Clients will receive direct notice. Continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.
