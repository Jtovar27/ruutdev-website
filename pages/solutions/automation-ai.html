Automation and AI

Automate a defined workflow, not a vague promise.

RuutDev scopes automation by the input it receives, the action it performs, the output it produces, and the point where a person reviews or takes over.

Practical cases

Quote request routing, form classification, assisted responses, task generation, operational summaries, and structured follow-up.

Human handoff

People review sensitive, ambiguous, high-impact, or exception cases. Automation should make that handoff visible and recoverable.

Defined limits

Access, data retention, failure modes, monitoring, and manual override are defined before anything goes live.
