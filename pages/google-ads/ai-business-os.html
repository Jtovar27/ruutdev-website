The Problem

Most small businesses run on a tired website and too many disconnected tools

Your website is your storefront. If it doesn't convert, you lose leads. If your CRM, scheduling, and content live in different apps, the owner pays the cost — in time, missed revenue, and decisions made too late.

Outdated website

Slow load, weak mobile, no clear CTA. Visitors bounce before they reach a contact form.

Leads are scattered

Form fills, DMs, calls, and walk-ins each end up somewhere different. Nothing is tied together.

Follow-ups get missed

Without a clear system, warm leads cool off and revenue quietly walks out the door.

Content is inconsistent

Posts go out in bursts, then nothing for weeks. The brand stops showing up where buyers are.

Scheduling is disconnected

Bookings live in one tool, customers in another, and reminders depend on someone remembering.

No clear focus

Owners often don't know what to focus on this week to actually move revenue.

Inside Your Build

Everything we wire into your project — so the website actually works for the business

Conversion-focused website

Mobile-first design, fast load, sharp CTA flow. Built so visitors actually become leads — not just impressions.

Use case: A med spa replaces its old slow site — bookings double in a month.

CRM + Leads

Capture leads from forms, calls, walk-ins, and ads in one inbox. Track contacts, deals, and follow-up cadences without spreadsheets.

Use case: A new website lead lands at 8pm — the CRM logs it and assigns a follow-up for tomorrow morning.

Online scheduling

Bookings on your site, customer reminders, internal availability, and a calendar that talks to your CRM. No double-bookings, no missed slots.

Use case: A barber takes appointments online and sends reminders automatically — phone calls drop.

Content workflow

Draft posts, captions, emails, and offers in one place. Reuse what works. AI helps with first drafts — you keep the voice.

Use case: A florist plans next week's posts in 20 minutes instead of every night.

Campaign tracking

Plan promos, launches, and seasonal pushes on a single timeline. See what's running, what's next, and what each campaign actually moved.

Use case: A restaurant runs a Mother's Day promo across email, social, and WhatsApp — all coordinated.

AI agents & automations

Specialized helpers for marketing, content, lead triage, and operations. They work behind the scenes and only surface decisions that need a human.

Use case: The lead-triage agent flags hot leads from cold ones so the owner only opens the right ones.

Payments & checkout

Stripe-ready flows for subscriptions, one-time products, deposits, and bookings — wired into your site without third-party redirects.

Use case: A coach sells session packs directly on the website — Stripe handles the rest.

Mobile-first delivery

Most of your visitors arrive on a phone. Every page is designed for thumbs first — touch targets, fast load, no horizontal scroll.

Use case: A real-estate agent shares the listing link in DMs — it loads instantly and converts.

SEO & analytics ready

Clean structure, schema markup, and analytics hooks so Google understands the site and so you can see what's working.

Use case: Local searches start finding the business within weeks of launch.
