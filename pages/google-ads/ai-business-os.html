The Problem

Most small businesses run on too many tools and not enough clarity

If lead follow-ups, scheduling, content, and campaigns live in different apps, it's the owner who pays the cost — in time, missed revenue, and decisions made too late.

Leads are scattered

Form fills, DMs, calls, and walk-ins each end up somewhere different. Nothing is tied together.

Follow-ups get missed

Without a clear system, warm leads cool off and revenue quietly walks out the door.

Content is inconsistent

Posts go out in bursts, then nothing for weeks. The brand stops showing up where buyers are.

Campaigns are hard to organize

It's hard to plan what to run, when to run it, and how to tell whether it actually worked.

Scheduling is disconnected

Bookings live in one tool, customers in another, and reminders depend on someone remembering.

No clear focus

Owners often don't know what to focus on this week to actually move revenue.

Inside Your Build

Everything we wire into your system — so you don't need ten apps

AI Business Dashboard

A single daily view of leads, bookings, tasks, and campaigns. AI surfaces what changed and what should happen today.

Use case: Open the system in the morning and see the 3 things to focus on today.

CRM + Leads

Capture leads from forms, calls, walk-ins, and ads in one inbox. Track contacts, deals, and follow-up cadences without spreadsheets.

Use case: A new website lead lands at 8pm — the CRM logs it and assigns a follow-up for tomorrow morning.

Scheduling

Online booking, customer reminders, internal availability, and a calendar that talks to your CRM. No double-bookings, no missed slots.

Use case: A med spa books appointments online and sends reminders automatically — phone calls drop.

Content Studio

Draft posts, captions, emails, and offers in one place. Reuse what works. AI helps with first drafts — you keep the voice.

Use case: A florist plans next week's posts in 20 minutes instead of every night.

Campaign Planner

Plan promos, launches, and seasonal pushes on a single timeline. See what's running, what's next, and what each campaign actually moved.

Use case: A restaurant runs a Mother's Day promo across email, social, and WhatsApp — all coordinated.

AI Agents

Specialized helpers for marketing, content, lead triage, and operations. They work behind the scenes and only surface decisions that need a human.

Use case: The lead-triage agent flags hot leads from cold ones so the owner only opens the right ones.

Operations & Tasks

A simple task system tied to leads, bookings, content, and campaigns — so things don't fall through the cracks.

Use case: 'Send invoice to Carla' isn't a Post-it on a fridge anymore.

Industry Recommendations

Each build is shaped to your business type — so a coach, a med spa, and a restaurant don't end up with the same generic system.

Use case: A real-estate agent gets follow-up cadences and content prompts tailored to listings.

Mobile Access

Designed for the phone first. Manage leads, view your schedule, and approve content on the go.

Use case: Reply to a new lead from a parking lot — no laptop needed.

FAQ

Honest answers before you book

What does RuutDev actually build?

RuutDev builds custom websites, software, and AI-powered systems for small businesses — including CRM tooling, scheduling flows, content workflows, campaign systems, and automations. Each project is built around how your business actually operates, not a one-size-fits-all template.

Is there a SaaS version coming?

Yes. RuutDev is developing an AI Business OS — a self-serve SaaS platform that combines CRM, scheduling, content, campaigns, and AI agents in one place. It is launching soon. Mention 'AI Business OS waitlist' when you book a call to be notified when early access opens.

Who is this for?

Small and medium business owners across many industries — local services, e-commerce, restaurants, real estate, coaches, agencies, insurance, and more — who want a clearer system for managing leads, marketing, scheduling, content, and operations.

Can you replace or upgrade my CRM?

Yes. We can build a CRM tailored to your workflow, or upgrade an existing one with AI-driven follow-ups, smarter lead capture, and better reporting. We pick the right path during the discovery call.

Can the system work on mobile?

Yes. Every system we build is mobile-first — you can manage leads, view your schedule, draft content, and act on AI recommendations from a phone or a desktop browser.

Does it work for my industry?

We work with florists, restaurants, med spas, real estate, coaches, agencies, insurance, local services, e-commerce, and event businesses. Each build is shaped around the way that industry actually operates.

How does pricing work?

Two models. Monthly Plans start at $149 setup + $45/month and include support and maintenance. Buyout (one-time) projects start at $800 for landing pages, $1,500 for business websites, $2,500+ for e-commerce or advanced custom builds, and custom quotes for full software / AI / automation systems.

How long does a project take?

Simple sites and starter builds typically launch within days. Larger custom systems and AI integrations are scoped during the call — most projects ship in weeks, not months.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. We support agencies that need a build partner for client projects, including white-label arrangements where appropriate. The upcoming AI Business OS SaaS will also include multi-workspace support for agencies managing multiple client accounts.
