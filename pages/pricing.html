FAQ

Questions clients usually ask before choosing

What does maintenance include on monthly plans?

Monthly plans include routine site maintenance, support around the selected plan scope, and minor ongoing edits. The exact cadence depends on the plan level, but the point is that support is already part of the relationship.

Can I start monthly and move into a buyout later?

Yes, that can be discussed when it makes commercial sense. The right path depends on the final scope, what has been built, and what ownership transfer needs to include.

When does monthly billing actually start?

The setup fee is handled first. Recurring billing starts when the site is live or formally handed off for launch, not while the project is still sitting in internal production.

How do buyout project payments work?

Buyout projects are quoted based on scope. For many projects the payment structure is phased around kickoff, progress, and final delivery. The exact structure is confirmed in writing before work begins.

Is the pricing page already prepared for Stripe Checkout?

Yes. The monthly plan buttons include Stripe-ready hook ids and safe fallbacks. Once live Checkout URLs or Payment Links are added to the site config, those CTAs can become direct purchase buttons without restructuring the page.
