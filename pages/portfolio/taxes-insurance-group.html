The Challenge

A GoDaddy site she was embarrassed to share

Mayte had a GoDaddy website she was actively embarrassed to share with clients. It was outdated, visually weak, and she was paying monthly for something that was doing more damage than good to her professional image. For a firm with 20+ years serving the Central Florida Hispanic community, the site didn't reflect the trust and expertise she had built.

The Solution

A complete ground-up redesign

A complete ground-up redesign. New visual identity with a professional color palette and typography that communicates credibility and warmth. Bilingual structure built for her Spanish-speaking client base. Clear service pages, lead capture flows, and a layout designed to turn visitors into inquiries.
