The Challenge

A single ticket page — nothing more

La Cafebreria had a single-purpose ticket sales page — nothing more. No brand presence, no way for customers to explore their menu, discover events, or engage with the experience before walking in the door. For a café and bookstore with a full cultural identity, that gap was costing them visibility and walk-ins.

The Solution

Built around how the business actually operates

A full-featured website built around how the business actually operates. Customers can browse the food and drink menu, view upcoming events and live shows, and place orders directly through the site. Behind the scenes, the team manages everything through a custom admin dashboard — updating menus, adding events, and controlling content without touching a line of code.
