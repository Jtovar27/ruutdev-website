Process

Clear decisions before code, validation before launch.

Every engagement is shaped by its approved scope. The process creates checkpoints for business context, UX, implementation, quality, and handoff.

01

Understand

Business goals, customer types, services, current process, and bilingual needs.

02

Scope

Deliverables, constraints, integrations, content responsibilities, and timeline.

03

Structure

Information architecture, forms, CTA paths, and operational workflow.

04

Build

Design, content, development, integrations, and practical automation where it fits.

05

Validate

Responsive QA, language review, accessibility checks, forms, events, and handoff details.

06

Launch and improve

Release, documentation, post-launch support path, and improvements based on real feedback.

Timeline discipline

The timeline follows the approved scope.

Dependencies, client inputs, third-party approvals, and review windows are identified before development begins.
