5 Project Process

Every project follows this standard process:

  • Discovery — you share your business information, goals, and preferences
  • Demo / Proposal — we build and present a demo with a detailed quote
  • Agreement — both parties confirm scope, timeline, and price in writing
  • Deposit — 50% payment received before development starts
  • Development — we build your site and share updates throughout
  • Review — you review the site and request revisions
  • Launch — final payment received, site goes live

Estimated timelines will be provided at the start of each project. Delays caused by late client feedback or missing materials may extend the timeline without penalty to RuutDev.

6 Revisions

Each project includes a defined number of revision rounds, agreed upon before work begins. Standard projects typically include:

A "revision round" means a batch of feedback addressed in one development cycle. Additional revision rounds beyond the agreed number will be quoted and billed separately at an hourly rate.

Revisions must be submitted in a single, consolidated list. RuutDev is not responsible for feedback submitted after the revision period has closed.
