Section 8

Project Process

The typical project lifecycle consists of the following stages:

  1. Discovery: Initial consultation to understand scope, goals, and requirements.
  2. Free Demo: A working prototype delivered within approximately 48 hours (for website projects).
  3. Proposal: A written proposal detailing scope, timeline, deliverables, and payment schedule.
  4. Agreement & Deposit: Client accepts the proposal and pays the initial deposit to commence work.
  5. Development: Active build phase. Client is available for feedback within reasonable timeframes.
  6. Review & Revisions: Client reviews deliverables and requests revisions within the included revision rounds.
  7. Launch & Final Payment: Project goes live; final payment milestone is due.

Any modifications to scope after the proposal is accepted may require a revised proposal and additional charges.

Section 9

Revisions

The number of included revision rounds is defined in the Client's proposal. As a general guideline:

  • Landing pages and websites: 2–3 rounds of revisions.
  • E-commerce stores: 3 rounds of revisions.
  • Software and custom systems: As defined in the project proposal.

A "revision round" consists of a consolidated set of feedback submitted in a single communication. Each round of revisions must be submitted before the next begins. Revisions that change the original scope may be billed as additional work.

Revisions requested after launch or after the included rounds are exhausted will be billed at RuutDev's hourly or project rate.
