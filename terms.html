1 About RuutDev

RuutDev offers: professional landing pages, e-commerce stores, AI-powered tools, custom dashboards, website redesigns, and free demo websites. The exact scope, deliverables, and pricing will be agreed upon in writing before work begins.

RuutDev offers a free demo website with no obligation to purchase. The demo is delivered within 48 hours, remains available for 30 days, and does not constitute a binding contract.

You are under no obligation to proceed with a paid project after receiving a free demo.

Every project follows: Discovery → Demo/Proposal → Agreement → Deposit → Development → Review → Launch. Timelines provided upfront. Delays from late client feedback do not penalize RuutDev.

Standard projects include 2 revision rounds (landing pages) or 3 rounds (e-commerce/complex). Additional rounds are billed separately. Feedback must be submitted in a single consolidated list per round.

Intellectual property does NOT transfer until full payment is received.

8 Client Responsibilities

The client agrees to: provide required content within the agreed timeframe, respond to feedback requests within 5 business days, ensure all provided content is legally owned or licensed, and not use the website for illegal or deceptive purposes.

9 Cancellations & Refunds

  • Before deposit — cancel anytime, no charge
  • After deposit, before work begins — full deposit refund
  • After work has begun — deposit is non-refundable. Work completed to that point may be billed proportionally
  • After delivery (Plan B or C) — installments already paid are non-refundable. Outstanding installments remain due per the agreed schedule
  • After site goes live — no refunds once the site is published and accessible to the public

In the event of client non-payment under Plan B or Plan C, RuutDev reserves the right to suspend the site, retain the domain and hosting credentials, and pursue recovery of the outstanding balance. RuutDev will provide written notice before taking any of these actions.

If RuutDev cancels a project without cause, the client will receive a full refund of all amounts paid.

RuutDev's total liability shall not exceed the total amount paid for the specific project. We are not liable for third-party service failures, content provided by the client that infringes third-party rights, or business results from the website.
