Payment types explained

  • Project Deposit: Paid upfront to begin work. Amount determined in your proposal (typically 33–50% of total).
  • Project Balance: Milestone or final payment. Amount and timing per your contract.
  • Monthly Maintenance: Recurring payment for your care plan ($97, $197, or $297/mo).
  • Invoice / Other: Any other payment with a specific reference number.

Project deposits are non-refundable after work begins. Maintenance plan payments are non-refundable for the current billing period. See our Terms & Conditions for full details including the cancellation notice requirement.
